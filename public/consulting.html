The AI employee does not simply chat over documents. It turns messy operational inputs into structured, reviewable work product.

A

Semantic fingerprinting

Groups records, emails, notes, and files into patient, workflow, payer, provider, or project-specific context.

B

C

Human-review outputs

Produces summaries, work queues, exception lists, draft responses, reconciliation notes, and audit trails for staff approval.

Security model: segmented, reviewable, and HIPAA-conscious

01. Source boundary

Collect with permissions

Data connectors are scoped by source, role, and workflow. Untrusted or broad sources can be limited to metadata until approved.

02. PHI boundary

Restrict identifiable data

PHI stays in a BAA-covered cloud or approved company device before any downstream AI work begins.

03. De-ID boundary

Scrub before AI

Non-BAA AI services receive only de-identified, minimized, or task-scoped data approved for that workflow.

04. Review boundary

Keep humans accountable

AI outputs are routed to staff for review before clinical, billing, patient, or operational consequences.
